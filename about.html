About Blood Link

Blood Link is a student project designed to demonstrate how technology can make blood donor searching easier. It provides a simple interface for entering donor information and searching demo registrations.

Our Mission

Our mission is to develop a user-friendly platform concept that connects people through technology and spreads awareness about blood donation.

Our Objectives

✔ Create a simple donor registration interface.

✔ Allow searching by blood group.

✔ Allow searching by city.

✔ Demonstrate information storage and retrieval.

✔ Promote awareness about blood donation.

Project Technology

Frontend: HTML and CSS

Programming: JavaScript

Storage: Browser Local Storage

Browser: Google Chrome

Our Vision

We aim to explore how a secure, verified and accessible online blood donor platform could help people find relevant donation information.
